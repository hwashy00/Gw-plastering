Plastering in Topsham

Topsham's a short hop down the estuary from Exeter, and we work there regularly.

Topsham's a proper old estuary port, and the buildings show it — Dutch-gabled merchant houses along the Strand, Georgian frontages, period cottages and a good few listed properties tucked through the conservation area. A lot of these older walls want a breathable lime plaster rather than modern gypsum; seal them up wrong and the damp's got nowhere to go. We know the difference and works sympathetically with older Topsham properties — while still turning out crisp, modern finishes on the newer homes around Retreat and the estuary side. Skimming, rendering, lime repairs or a full re-plaster, all covered locally.

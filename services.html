Common questions

Which areas do you cover?
Exeter and the surrounding towns and villages: Topsham, Pinhoe, Broadclyst, Exmouth, Crediton, Tiverton, Cullompton, Ottery St Mary, Newton Abbot and Okehampton, plus lime and render work further across Devon. If you're not sure whether you're in reach, just ask.
Do you charge for a quote?
No. Quotes are free, and you get a fixed price before any work starts.
Can you quote from photos?
Usually, yes. Send a few photos on WhatsApp and we can give you a price or a sensible ballpark without anyone having to come out. Bigger or more complicated jobs are better seen in person.
Will you take on a small job?
Yes. A single wall, one ceiling or a patch repair is fine, we don't only take on full houses.

Not sure which one you need?

Send a photo of the wall, ceiling or elevation and you'll get a straight answer on what it needs, and a price.

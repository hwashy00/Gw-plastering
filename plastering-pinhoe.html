Good to know

Common questions

Do you cover Pinhoe?
Yes, and often — it's effectively Exeter, so we're in and out of there constantly. No travel charge and usually quick to get to you.
What's the typical Pinhoe job?
Mostly two things. There's the older housing stock around the village where people are renovating — re-skims, ceilings, blown plaster. And there's the newer estate housing towards Monkerton and Tithebarn, where it's usually boarding and skimming for extensions, loft conversions and knocking rooms together.
Can you skim over artex without removing it?
Sometimes, if it's a shallow pattern and it's sound. If it's a heavy swirl or it's flaking, it needs to come off first. One thing worth knowing: artex from before the 2000s can contain asbestos. If yours is that old, it should be tested before anyone starts scraping at it.

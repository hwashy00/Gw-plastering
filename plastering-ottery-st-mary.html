Plastering in Ottery St Mary

Ottery and the east-Devon villages around it are well within our patch.

Ottery St Mary's a historic little market town, and there's a lot of old fabric in it — cob and thatch cottages, rendered stone, and period terraces around the church, alongside the newer estates on the edges. Cob and older solid walls really need a breathable lime; modern gypsum traps moisture and does more harm than good over time. We're comfortable working with these traditional materials as well as standard skimming and rendering, so whether it's a period cottage that needs sympathetic repair or a modern room that wants a clean skim, it's covered — done tidily and without cutting corners.

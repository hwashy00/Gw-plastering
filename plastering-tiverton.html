Plastering in Tiverton

Tiverton and the mid-Devon countryside around it are well within reach, an area we cover regularly.

Tiverton's a proper old market town, and it shows in the buildings. Plenty of stone and cob cottages, farmhouses out in the villages, and older terraces near the centre, alongside the newer estates up around Post Hill and Blundell's. The older properties often want a breathable lime plaster rather than modern gypsum, especially where damp's been an issue; get that wrong and the wall can't dry out. We know the difference and won't seal up a wall that needs to breathe.

Out in the more exposed spots, external rendering takes a beating from the weather rolling in off the hills. Re-rendering or patching tired render keeps the wet out and smartens the place up at the same time, and it's work we do plenty of around here.

Common questions

Do you cover Tiverton from Exeter?
Yes, regularly. Tiverton is roughly fifteen miles north and it's well within our normal working area. No travel surcharge, and we can usually get up for a look within a few days.
What sort of plastering work comes up most in Tiverton?
A lot of it is Victorian terraces around the town centre and the old mill areas, blown plaster on solid walls, lath and plaster ceilings that have started to sag, and re-skims during renovations. There's also newer estate housing on the edges, which is usually straightforward boarding and skimming.
My Tiverton ceiling is lath and plaster and it's sagging. Can it be saved?
Sometimes. If the plaster has come away from the laths across most of the ceiling, it's usually more sensible and no more expensive to take it down and re-board. If it's a localised area and the rest is sound, it can often be repaired. We'll tell you honestly which one you're looking at.

Why K-Rend

K-Rend is one of the UK's most trusted silicone render systems — and for good reason. On Devon's exposed, damp-prone properties, the right render is the difference between a facade that still looks sharp in ten years and one that's streaked, cracked and tired.

Through-coloured, so no repainting. The colour runs all the way through the render rather than sitting on top as paint. That means no repainting every few years, no patchy fading, and no peeling — the finish you get on day one is the finish you keep.

Water-repellent but breathable. Silicone render sheds rain from the surface while still letting the wall breathe underneath. That combination is exactly what Devon's wet, mild climate demands — it keeps driving rain out without trapping moisture in the wall.

Self-cleaning and low maintenance. The hydrophobic surface means rain runs off carrying dirt with it, so K-Rend stays cleaner for longer than cement render or painted finishes — a real advantage on north and west-facing walls where algae and green growth take hold.

A clean, modern look. Whether it's a crisp white new build or a warmer through-colour on a period property, K-Rend gives a flat, contemporary finish that lifts the whole look of a property — and adds real value.

The K-Rend systems We apply

Every property and substrate is different — the right K-Rend product depends on the wall you're starting with. We work across the full range and will spec the correct system for your job.

Silicone K1 — Scraped Texture

The classic scraped-texture silicone render, applied over a base coat onto brick or blockwork. A durable, through-coloured finish in a wide range of standard colours — the go-to for most whole-house and new-build jobs.

Silicone TC — Thin Coat

A thinner, ready-mixed silicone top coat, ideal over insulation boards and cement board on studwork. Hand or spray applied for a smooth, even finish where flexibility over the substrate matters.

K Mono — Monocouche

A one-coat, through-coloured monocouche render — a fast, attractive finish that's very common on Exeter new builds. Great where a clean single-coat solution suits the property and budget.

Dash & Dry Dash Receiver

For traditional dry-dash and pebbledash finishes, or refurbishing existing dashed walls — the silicone dash receiver gives the modern weatherproofing benefits behind a classic textured look.

Spray-Applied Systems

For larger elevations and new builds, spray application lays the render down quickly and evenly across big areas — the efficient route on whole-property and multi-elevation jobs.

Base Coats & Preparation

A render is only as good as what's under it. The right base coat, beading and preparation are what stop cracking and give the top coat something sound to key into — never skipped, never rushed.

K-Rend questions

Do I ever need to repaint K-Rend?

No — silicone render is through-coloured, so the colour runs all the way through rather than sitting on the surface as paint. There's no repainting every few years and no patchy fading, which is one of its biggest advantages over painted cement render.

Can you K-Rend over my existing pebbledash or render?

Often, yes — it depends on the condition of what's already there. If the existing surface is sound it can usually be rendered over after the right preparation; if it's blown or loose it may need taking back first. We'll tell you which after a proper look, rather than guessing.

Why is silicone render good for Devon weather?

Devon's climate is wet and mild — perfect conditions for algae, damp and streaking on exteriors. Silicone render is water-repellent yet breathable, so it sheds driving rain from the surface while letting the wall breathe underneath. The self-cleaning surface also helps keep green growth at bay, especially on shaded, north-facing walls.

What colours does K-Rend come in?

There's a wide range of standard through-colours, from classic whites and off-whites through to warmer stone and grey tones — plus bespoke colours on some systems. We'll help you pick a colour that suits the property and its surroundings.

What does K-Rend cost?

It costs more than painted cement render up front. Here's why it usually works out cheaper.

You never paint it

K-Rend is through-coloured — the colour goes all the way through. Painted cement render needs redoing every five years or so, scaffold and all. That adds up fast.

It lasts decades

A properly applied silicone render should go 20–30 years before it needs anything doing to it.

Scaffolding once

The big hidden cost of repainting render isn't the paint — it's putting the scaffold back up every time. Through-coloured render means you do that once.

What moves the price

Size of the property, scaffolding, access, how much prep the existing wall needs, and the amount of detailing around windows and corners.

Re-render vs new build

New blockwork is a clean, quick job. Taking an old failing render off first adds days and skips.

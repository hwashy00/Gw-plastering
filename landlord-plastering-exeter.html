Landlord plastering questions

Straight answers, no waffle.

How quickly can you turn a property round between tenants?

Most single-room jobs are done in a day, and a whole flat re-skim is usually two to four days depending on size. We know void periods cost you money, so we work to your changeover dates where we can.

Can you work around tenants still living there?

Yes. We keep the work contained to one room at a time, sheet everything up and clear up properly at the end of each day. It is not ideal for anyone, but it is very doable for repairs and single rooms.

Do you invoice letting agents directly?

Yes. We work with several Exeter agents and are happy to invoice the agency rather than the landlord, whichever suits your setup.

Is plastering the landlord's responsibility?

Generally yes. Under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985, the landlord is responsible for keeping the structure and interior of the property in repair, which covers walls and ceilings. Damage caused by the tenant is a separate matter and may be recoverable from the deposit.

What affects the cost for a rental property?

For a landlord, the void week usually costs more than the plastering does.
